# Pilot Intake Questionnaire

## Company and team
1. Company size and finance team size?
2. Who owns month-end close?
3. Who signs off on workflow/process changes?

## Current workflow
4. Which systems are used in close/reconciliation today?
5. Where are the biggest manual bottlenecks?
6. What currently causes delays or rework?

## API and integration readiness
7. Which systems have usable APIs today?
8. Any strict rate limits or token-scoping limitations?
9. Any data residency or network constraints?

## Governance and controls
10. Which actions must always require approval?
11. What audit evidence is mandatory?
12. Who can authorize policy changes?

## Pilot success definition
13. What measurable outcome would make this pilot successful?
14. What timeline is acceptable for proving value?
15. What would block broader rollout even if pilot works?
